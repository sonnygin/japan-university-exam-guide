# Quality Checklist

Use this checklist before delivering the final proposal. Revise any failed item instead of ignoring it.

## 1. Professor Alignment

- Is the proposed topic clearly aligned with the professor's **recent** research trajectory?
- Does the proposal rely on at least 2 representative recent papers from the professor or lab where available?
- Has any explicit "not accepting students" statement been surfaced to the user if found?
- Are all professor-related factual claims traceable to real sources?

## 2. Applicant Fit

- Does the proposal reflect the applicant's actual strengths rather than generic claims?
- Is the methodology realistic for the applicant's current training level?
- Does the proposal explain why the applicant can plausibly execute the project?

## 3. Topic Quality

- Is the topic concrete rather than broad or fashionable?
- Is there a visible research gap?
- Is the central research question answerable with the proposed methods?
- Is the scope suitable for the target degree level?

## 4. Literature Quality

- Does the literature review synthesize sources instead of listing them mechanically?
- Are both foundational and recent sources included where relevant?
- Do the cited works directly support background, gap, or method claims?
- Are there enough references for the requested depth?
- For PhD-level proposals, are there roughly 40 or more references unless the user requested otherwise?

## 5. Structural Quality

- Does the proposal include the expected core sections?
- Is the logic clear from problem to gap to method to contribution?
- Are transitions between sections smooth and explicit?
- Does the abstract accurately reflect the final draft?

## 6. Methodology Quality

- Is the method described clearly enough to seem executable?
- Does the proposal explain why the selected method fits the research question?
- Are data, materials, experiments, or analytical procedures sufficiently specified?
- Are evaluation criteria or validation logic included where needed?
- Are limitations or feasibility constraints acknowledged when appropriate?

## 7. Writing Quality

- Is the proposal written primarily in connected prose rather than fragmented bullets?
- Is the tone academically serious and specific?
- Are generic praise and exaggerated novelty claims avoided?
- Are figure suggestions included in the required format when appropriate?

## 8. Citation and Factual Integrity

- Are all references real and non-fabricated?
- Is citation style internally consistent?
- Are uncertain facts verified or clearly marked before delivery?
- Are paper titles, venues, years, and names spelled consistently?

## 9. Language Consistency

- Is the full document written in the user-requested language?
- Are paper titles kept in their original language in references?
- Is the language natural and discipline-appropriate throughout?

## 10. Deliverable Readiness

- Is the final file cleanly formatted in Markdown?
- Are section headings readable and consistent?
- Can the document be directly edited or exported after delivery?
- Has the proposal passed both confirmation gates if the workflow required them?

## Release Rule

Deliver the proposal only when the document is:
1. factually grounded,
2. structurally coherent,
3. aligned with both professor and applicant,
4. and polished enough for serious user review.
